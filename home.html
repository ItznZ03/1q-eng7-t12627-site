Forest to be Brought Back

A poem by Group 5 of 7 - Chabanel

Core Theme

“We bring the forest back to life, and find our own lives healed.”

When we plant mangroves and bring the forest back to life, we heal our own lives in return. Planting mangroves greatly protects coastal communities by preventing floods, nourishing marine life and local food sources, and restoring the overall environment.

Stanza 1 • Identity of the Partner Community

Coastal fishermen move through the heavy silt, Sheltering roots where young mangroves are built. Strong coastal trees form a shield by the shore, United by teamwork to guard it forevermore.

Jose Charito III Cortez
Stanza 2 • Challenges / Issues

Plastic scattered along the shore, Threatening what feeds them more and more, My heart grew at the sight, Yet hope still shines to guide their fight.

Raven Daniela Chu
Stanza 3 • Lessons, Reflection, Values Learned

As we help improve the biodiversity, It makes our lives more easy. Planting trees help against the calamity, Enhancing the mentality of humanity.

Lianne Mangao
Stanza 4 • Reflection

In Matina Aplaya they fall down but never give up, Planting mangroves so the challenges can't keep up. A friend once said, "One tree grows a forest, that's how we start." Their resilience made me realize strength grows from our hearts.

Christorey Eshan Marco
Stanza 5 • Call to Action or Commitment

In nature’s beauty horror lies. Yet care transforms the fear before our eyes. Harm leaves us broken, but true help will heal. For in the end, nature’s power is real.

Cristan Euan A. Castaneda
